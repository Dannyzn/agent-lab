# South shipping policy

This document is the authoritative regional rate card. All money is in integer cents.

## Operational note 1
Weights are measured in grams at dispatch. Do not convert the quote to floating-point currency. Customer subtotals exclude shipping charges. Delivery estimates and operational notes do not change the numeric rate card. The quote must be deterministic; no network lookup or account configuration is required.
